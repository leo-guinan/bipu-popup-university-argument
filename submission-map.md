The packet says completions are judged with documentation/proof-of-work. This map avoids turning one artifact into several fictional completions.

| Packet mechanism | Literal fit | Spirit fit | Evidence | Recommended position |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| E / Make E Great Again | User-supplied | Strong as a translation | Exact token/publication receipt missing | Describe as an implementation claim, not an outcome |

## Proposed judge-facing package

One packet with four sections:

1. **The protocol** — the List's repeated loop: scope, build, distribute, document, judge.
2. **The translations** — book, thread, landing page, symbolic artifacts, Roots of a Nation, HumAIn Society, E, sponsor proof-of-play, journalist rail.
3. **The receipts** — URLs, hashes, provider IDs, timestamps, deployment evidence, and explicit readback methods.
4. **The limits** — no invented reach, readership, impact, endorsement, token value, attendance, or score.

## Requested judge decision

Do not ask the judges to accept every implementation as a separate high-value stunt. Ask them to judge whether the metaplur demonstrates the protocol more completely than isolated literal completions:

- Does one idea remain legible across the translations?
- Is each translation shipped and inspectable?
- Is the provenance chain intact?
- Are outcomes distinguished from distribution?
- Does the work reduce the time from unfamiliar source material to public, receipted implementation?

That is the actual case. It is less noisy than claiming 76 completions, and therefore more likely to survive contact with a skeptical judge.
