# The List as a Protocol

Argument repository for the Netwar Con submission.

## Thesis

The List is not merely a checklist of 76 isolated stunts. It is a deliberately theatrical protocol for making information work legible: identify a target or question, scope an intervention, build an artifact, distribute it through an information medium, document proof-of-work, and submit the result for judgment.

Our case is that the strongest completion is therefore a metaplur: one operating idea translated across several concrete implementations without pretending that reach is impact or that an artifact is an outcome.

The implementations include:

- a source-bounded book and public evidence repository;
- a verified six-post X narrative thread;
- a live landing page and lead magnet;
- 20 symbolic territory artifacts;
- a journalist-discovery and consent-separated outreach pipeline;
- 11 individually reviewed journalist messages accepted by the transactional provider;
- the Roots of a Nation interpretive site;
- a HumAIn Society identity and account, reported by the builder but not independently read back here;
- the E / Make E Great Again implementation, reported by the builder but awaiting an exact public receipt;
- sponsor proof-of-play, reported by the builder but awaiting exact post or artifact URLs.

The event-level frame is a first Build In Public University popup: a temporary, distributed learning environment that showed people new ideas and current production possibilities in a non-threatening way. “Accelerated futures” is the mechanism we are arguing for; participant-level impact still requires participant-level evidence.

The argument is not that these items automatically score. It is that they demonstrate the List's underlying protocol across different surfaces, while preserving source boundaries and outcome uncertainty.

## Evidence posture

- `evidence-ledger.md` separates verified artifacts, provider acceptance, user-supplied claims, and unknown outcomes.
- `argument.md` makes the persuasive case without converting claims into facts.
- `submission-map.md` maps the work to the spirit and letter of the packet.
- `claims/receipt-gaps.md` lists what must be added before any public or judge-facing claim is upgraded.
- `journalist-outreach.md` records the 11 provider-accepted messages, their exact public-safe copy, and the evidence boundary.
- `receipts/` contains copies of local, non-secret receipt artifacts.

No secrets, private journalist addresses, or raw participant contact data belong in this repository.

## Core boundary

No wrongdoing is alleged. This is an evidence-backed account of preparation, translation, distribution, and documentation. It does not prove event attendance, judge acceptance, audience reach, behavioral change, cryptocurrency performance, journalist readership, or sponsor endorsement.
